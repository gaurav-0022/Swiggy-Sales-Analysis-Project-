
# 🍔 Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard

## 1.Swiggy-Sales-Analysis-Project-

**🍔 Swiggy Sales Analysis: India Food Delivery Insights Dashboard**
A dynamic and interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Swiggy’s sales performance across India, focusing on revenue trends, customer ratings, food preferences, and regional demand.

---

## 2. Short Description / Purpose

The Swiggy Sales Analysis Dashboard is a visually rich Power BI report designed to help analysts and stakeholders understand sales growth, order behavior, and customer satisfaction across cities and states in India. It supports data-driven decisions related to operations, marketing, and expansion.

---

## 3. Tech Stack

The dashboard was built using the following tools and technologies:<br>
• 📊 **Power BI Desktop** – Core platform for building interactive reports and visuals.<br>
• 📂 **Power Query** – Used for data cleaning, transformation, and shaping.<br>
• 🧠 **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Created calculated measures for KPIs, trends, and comparisons.<br>
• 🧩 **Data Modeling** – Established relationships between sales, time, food category, restaurant, and geography tables.<br>
• 📁 **File Format** – `.pbix` for development and `.png` for dashboard preview.

---

## 4. Data Source

**Source:** Simulated Swiggy business dataset (for analytical and portfolio purposes)

The dataset contains:

* Order-level sales and revenue data
* Customer ratings and rating counts
* Food type and category details (Veg / Non‑Veg)
* Restaurant information
* City and state-wise geographic data
* Time dimensions (daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly)

This structure enables multi-level analysis from overall KPIs to granular regional insights.

---

## 5. Features / Highlights

### • Business Problem

Food delivery platforms like Swiggy operate at massive scale, generating large volumes of transactional data. Without a consolidated analytics view, it is difficult to track performance trends, identify high-performing regions, and understand customer preferences.

Key business questions include:

* How are sales and orders trending over time?
* Which cities and states generate the highest revenue?
* What is the contribution of Veg vs Non‑Veg food?
* How do ratings and order values reflect customer satisfaction?

---

### • Goal of the Dashboard

To deliver an interactive analytical tool that:

* Monitors overall sales, orders, and customer ratings
* Identifies top-performing cities, states, and restaurants
* Analyzes food-type preferences and order value trends
* Supports strategic planning and business optimization

---

**Trend Analysis**

* **Weekly Sales Trend (Column Chart):** Displays sales consistency across weeks.

**Category & Food Insights**

* **Sales by Food Type (Donut Chart):** Comparison between Veg and Non‑Veg sales contribution.

**Geographical Insights**

* **Sales by State (Map):** Visualizes revenue distribution across Indian states.
* **Top 5 Cities by Sales (Bar Chart):** Bengaluru, Lucknow, Hyderabad, Mumbai, and New Delhi.

**Quarterly Performance Table**

* Consolidated view of **Sales, Ratings, and Orders** across Q1–Q3 for quick comparison.

---

### • Business Impact & Insights

* **Regional Strategy:** Identifies high-revenue states and cities for targeted expansion.
* **Customer Experience Analysis:** Ratings and order value help evaluate service quality.
* **Category Optimization:** Reveals customer preference trends for Veg vs Non‑Veg food.
* **Operational Planning:** Supports demand forecasting using daily and weekly patterns.
